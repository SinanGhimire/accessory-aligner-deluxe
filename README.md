# Accessory Aligner Deluxe

What I found: the idle art is a single frame (so gear can't follow an idle bob), and the head positions used to place hats were measured too wide — that's why the accessories look oversized and drift out of sync while your character moves. What remains: re-measure each hero's head position frame by frame from the actual artwork, shrink the gear to match the real head width, and give the death pose its own placement so hats don't float.
